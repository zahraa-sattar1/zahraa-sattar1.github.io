Sensor Information

Data acquisition system details

BH1750

PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation), measured in lux (lx), profiled through 0–100 m depth. Represents visible light intensity (400–700 nm) in the water column.

TSL2591 + 850nm IR LED

Optical backscatter, measured in m⁻¹ (bbp). The IR LED emits at 850 nm and the TSL2591 detects the light scattered back by suspended particles — serves as a proxy for particulate concentration / turbidity.

Adafruit LPS35HW

Pressure in hPa (mbar), converted to depth in metres (m) via hydrostatic pressure calculation. Also measures water temperature in °C.

Arduino Nano 33 IoT

Microcontroller handling data acquisition from all sensors via I2C bus. Interfaces with LoRa transceiver for remote transmission.
